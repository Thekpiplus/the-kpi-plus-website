## From activity to a more accountable commercial route.
Useful when a hotel wants to explore technology, automation, or AI through practical workflows rather than disconnected tools or vague innovation claims.
01
### Start with the operating question
Clarify the repeated task, information gap, handoff, or decision that is creating unnecessary manual work or commercial uncertainty.
02
### Assess practical opportunities
Consider appropriate process, integration, automation, or AI-assisted options in the context of the hotel’s data, team, and operating constraints.
03
### Keep accountability visible
Define what remains a human decision, how outputs are checked, who owns the workflow, and what the team should review after adoption.
