Google Ads management connects search intent, campaign structure, landing-page relevance, conversion measurement, and commercial review so paid demand is not separated from the hotel’s wider booking path.

## From activity to a more accountable commercial route.
Useful when Google Ads needs a more accountable relationship with the hotel’s direct-booking and revenue priorities.
01
### Define the demand question
Start with the market, period, offer, guest intent, and booking-path question the campaign should help the hotel answer.
02
### Connect ad and landing experience
Align campaign language with a relevant landing page, useful proof, and a clear next action rather than sending demand into a generic path.
03
### Review the commercial signal
Use consistent attribution and conversion signals to understand what is generating meaningful enquiries or booking movement.
