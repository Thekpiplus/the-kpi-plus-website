## A connected system can still be set up incorrectly.
A PMS, Channel Manager, or Booking Engine can be technically connected while room mapping, rate structures, OTA pricing, restrictions, inventory logic, and reporting still make daily hotel decisions harder than they need to be.
We understand hotel systems from both sides: how the technology works and how the hotel needs to sell.
