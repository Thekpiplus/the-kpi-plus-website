We speak with the owner, the GM, the commercial team, and the front line in the context of each role, because we come from this industry and know who needs which information, and when.

If a tool cuts repetitive work, gets data to the team faster, and frees time for revenue and guests, we use it. If it does not help the team decide or work better, we don't.

Frequently asked questions
### Which types of hotels is this for?
Independent hotels, boutiques, and more complex operations in Thailand, where the owner, GM, or commercial team wants to look at revenue, pricing, and channels themselves. It does not have to be a large chain.
### What information should we prepare?
Start with the hotel name, province, an approximate room count, and what is worrying you now — such as occupancy, pricing, or OTAs. You do not need a full report on day one. The team will ask for more when you talk.
### Do we have to connect our systems first?
No. The team looks at the data and systems the hotel already has, then proposes a way of working that fits the size and readiness of the hotel. You do not have to change the PMS or RMS before the first conversation.
### How do you work with our existing Revenue team?
We do not replace the hotel's Revenue team. We look at the numbers together, separate the leak from the opportunity, and leave the hotel team with something they can act on.
### How can you help with OTAs and direct bookings?
We look at the mix of OTA and direct bookings, commission, and rates on each channel, then help decide what to adjust first so the hotel is not relying on one channel too heavily.
### How long does it take to get started?
You can send the form now; it takes about two minutes. The team replies within two business days. After a conversation and a look at the information you have, they summarise what to focus on first. The full scope of work does not start on day one.
### Is hotel information kept confidential?
Yes. Contact and review details are kept confidential and used to follow up on this request, as described in the privacy policy. We do not publish a case without permission.
