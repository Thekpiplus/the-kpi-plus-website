# Local SEO for Hotels: Make Google Maps and Your Business Profile Drive Bookings (2026 Update)

**Local SEO for hotels** is the work that makes your hotel appear on Google Maps and in local search results when travellers look for a stay near a place they care about. Visibility turns into bookings only when your details are accurate, reviews answer guests' doubts, your website explains rooms and location well, and booking links show prices that match reality.

This guide is for owners, GMs and sales teams at independent hotels in Thailand. The question we answer: **how does Google rank hotels on the map, what must we maintain on our Business Profile, and how do we measure whether it brings bookings?**

**Key points:** Google ranks local results by relevance, distance and prominence · Hotel details must be complete and match your website and OTAs · Fresh reviews beat many old ones · Hotels can't post discounts on their profile · Prices and booking links come from Hotel Center, not the profile

## What changed in 2026: a hotel-specific playbook, and AI recommending stays

In 2026 Google published Business Profile playbooks by industry, including one for hotels and accommodation. It says hotels can edit their hotel details and amenities directly, and that hotels cannot create offer posts or posts mentioning deals, promotions or discounts.

![Three changes affecting local SEO for hotels in 2026: Google's Business Profile playbook for hotels, 45% of consumers using AI to find local businesses, and hotel booking in Google's AI Mode in the United States](/images/blog/hotel-local-seo/en/what-changed.webp)

BrightLocal's Local Consumer Review Survey 2026 found 74% of consumers only care about reviews from the last three months, and its March 2026 follow-up reported that people using AI tools to find local businesses rose from 6% to 45% in a year. Since 27 August 2026, Google has let users in the United States search for and book hotels in AI Mode, drawing on Google Maps details such as photos and reviews.

**What this means for hotels:** your Google Maps information isn't only shown on the map — AI uses it to describe and recommend hotels. A complete, accurate profile with fresh reviews matters more than ever.

## What is local SEO for hotels, and how does Google rank local results?

Google says local results depend on three main factors. Hotels control two of them; distance depends on where the searcher is.

![The three factors Google uses in local SEO for hotels: relevance to the search, distance from the searcher or searched place, and prominence from reviews, mentions and website](/images/blog/hotel-local-seo/en/three-factors.webp)

| Factor | Meaning | What the hotel can do |
|---|---|---|
| Relevance | How well your details match the search | Category, hotel details, amenities, website pages |
| Distance | How close you are to the searcher or place searched | Accurate pin; write honestly about nearby places |
| Prominence | How well known the business is | Reviews, local mentions, a trustworthy website |

## 1. Check ownership and complete your Business Profile

Start by confirming the hotel actually owns the profile, has more than one manager, and every basic detail is right.

![Hotel Business Profile checklist: ownership and identity (owner, category, name-address-phone) and hotel details (amenities, check-in and check-out times, website link)](/images/blog/hotel-local-seo/en/gbp-checklist.webp)

- **Access:** the hotel owns the profile, has a backup manager, and has removed former staff or agencies
- **Identity:** the real hotel name with no added keywords; category "Hotel" or the closest match
- **Hotel details:** amenities, check-in and check-out times, star rating if any, and website link

**Watch out:** never add keywords to your hotel name — e.g. "[Hotel name] – Cheap Hotel Near Beach". Google treats it as a guideline breach and may suspend the profile.

## 2. Show your location and the details guests use to decide

Profile photos should answer the same questions as your website: what the entrance looks like, what each room type is like, and the real view. Add new photos after renovations, and check that user-uploaded photos still reflect reality.

**Do this:** photograph the entrance, lobby, every room type, bathrooms and main facilities, upload them together, and schedule fresh photos every quarter.

## 3. Ask every guest for a review, and reply responsibly

Reviews are part of the prominence Google uses for local ranking, and guests read them before deciding. Ask every guest with the same message, offer nothing in return, and reply to good and bad reviews consistently.

The KPI Plus [Google review link generator (in Thai)](/tools/hotel-review-link) creates a link and QR code for your post-stay message.

**Numbers to watch:** new reviews per month · 90-day average rating · % of reviews answered

## 4. Make your website answer local searches — then lead to booking

Google uses your website to understand which area you're relevant to. A good location page gives real distances to the beach, key neighbourhoods and the airport, with an easy booking button. Your name, address and phone on the website must match the profile exactly.

## 5. Build local relevance from real relationships

Mentions from local sources build prominence — your provincial hotel association, partner restaurants and activity providers, local festivals and regional travel media. Keep your hotel's details identical everywhere.

![Four local SEO signals hotels can build: consistent name, address and phone everywhere, local partners, location pages on the website, and a steady flow of fresh reviews](/images/blog/hotel-local-seo/en/local-signals.webp)

## 6. Separate Google Maps from prices and booking links

Your profile helps guests find you, but prices and booking links in Google's hotel panel come from Google Hotel Center. To show your direct rate next to the OTAs, connect free booking links through a booking engine or channel manager that is a Google partner.

**Do this:** ask your booking engine provider whether it connects to Google Hotel Center, and check the rate you send includes tax and service the same way your booking page does.

## 7. Measure from discovery to booking

Business Profile performance reports show how many people saw your profile, which searches found you, and how many called, requested directions or visited your website. Read them alongside Google-sourced bookings in your booking engine.

![Four local SEO for hotels metrics: profile views and search terms, website clicks, calls and direction requests, and bookings from Google](/images/blog/hotel-local-seo/en/gbp-metrics.webp)

**Numbers to watch:** monthly profile views · search terms that found you · website and booking-link clicks · Google-sourced bookings in your engine

## Frequently asked questions

### Will local SEO get our hotel into the top three on the map?

No one can guarantee it. Google ranks local results by relevance, distance from the searcher and the business's prominence. You can't change distance. What you can do is keep information complete and consistent, earn fresh reviews steadily and build pages that answer local searches — which improves your chances for relevant searches.

### If we complete our Business Profile, will a direct booking link appear?

Not automatically. Prices and booking links in Google's hotel panel come from Google Hotel Center, not from the profile alone. To use free booking links you must connect rates through a booking engine or channel manager that is a Google partner, and those rates must match your booking page.

### Do hotels need to post on their Business Profile every day?

No. Post updates or events when there's something guests should know — a new restaurant, a festival activity. Under Google's guidelines, hotels can't create offer posts or posts about discounts. Accurate hotel details, photos and fresh reviews matter far more than posting often.

### How much OTA commission can local SEO save?

It depends on where guests who find you on Google Maps end up booking. If your profile has a direct booking link and your rate matches the OTAs, some will book direct, and each booking moved from an OTA saves that booking's commission. Track booking-link clicks from your profile and Google-sourced bookings in your engine.

### How is local SEO for hotels different from website SEO?

Website SEO helps your pages rank in organic results; local SEO helps your hotel appear on Google Maps and in the local results panel. They connect: Google uses your website to understand local relevance, and guests who see you on the map usually visit your website before booking.
