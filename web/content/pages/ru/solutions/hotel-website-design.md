# Give the hotel website a clearer commercial job.
Hotel website design connects information hierarchy, guest confidence, mobile usability, booking-path clarity, and brand expression so the website can support a more useful commercial journey.
Revenue · Direct Booking · Commercial Growth
Connect revenue, direct booking, and commercial-growth priorities so the hotel team can choose the next move and measure what changes.

What the work is designed to clarify
## From activity to a more accountable commercial route.
Useful when a hotel needs its website to explain its value more clearly, guide guests with less friction, and support direct commercial action.
01
### Map the guest decision path
Identify what a prospective guest needs to understand, compare, and do next across the most important pages and devices.
02
### Design a clearer hierarchy
Organise pages, proof, visual context, and calls to action around the decision the website should help a guest make.
03
### Connect design and conversion
Keep the design connected to booking paths, measurable actions, and the wider commercial system rather than treating it as an isolated visual project.
