## From activity to a more accountable commercial route.
Useful when a hotel needs commercial processes, systems, or new priorities to translate into practical team confidence and accountable day-to-day action.
01
### Identify the capability need
Clarify the commercial decision, operating handoff, or repeated task where the team needs more shared context, confidence, or role clarity.
02
### Build practical learning into work
Shape training around the hotel’s real workflows, tools, responsibilities, and questions rather than generic content disconnected from daily action.
03
### Create an ownership rhythm
Set clear owners, review points, and reinforcement so the new capability can become part of the operating rhythm after the initial learning session.
