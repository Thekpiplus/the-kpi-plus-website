## From activity to a more accountable commercial route.
Useful when a hotel has website or campaign traffic but needs a more deliberate path from interest to informed enquiry or direct booking.
01
### Review the current path
Map how a guest arrives, what information is available, where confidence is needed, and where the path to enquiry or booking becomes unclear.
02
### Prioritise high-value changes
Focus on practical page, message, call-to-action, or handoff improvements that address the journey most relevant to current commercial demand.
03
### Measure the useful action
Connect changes to agreed enquiry, booking-path, or conversion signals so the team can review whether the journey is becoming clearer.
